# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads define and maintain system architecture, make technical strategy decisions, and ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with organizational standards. They work closely with Developers and Project Managers to balance technical excellence with business constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Evaluate technical approaches and recommend solutions that align with organizational standards
- Review architectural designs and code for scalability and maintainability
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and technical debt
- Guide developers on best practices, design patterns, and coding standards
- Participate in design reviews, spike investigations, and technology evaluations
- Collaborate with Product Managers on feasibility and technical trade-offs

### Goals
- Ensure long-term system health and scalability
- Reduce technical debt and improve system reliability
- Enable developers to deliver high-quality solutions efficiently
- Align technical strategy with business objectives

### Typical Communication
- Design review meetings and architecture discussions
- Technical decision logs and ADRs (Architecture Decision Records)
- Sprint planning and estimation support
- Mentoring and code review feedback
- Technical presentations to stakeholders when needed

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Provides technical guidance, reviews code, and mentors on best practices
- **Project Managers**: Advises on technical feasibility and timeline implications of architectural decisions
- **Product Managers**: Collaborates on feature feasibility and technical trade-offs affecting delivery

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality strategy, define acceptance criteria validation approaches, and ensure features meet quality standards before release. They partner with Developers and Product Managers to embed quality throughout the development lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define QA strategy and comprehensive test plan for each initiative
- Create and maintain acceptance criteria with Product and Development teams
- Conduct or oversee manual QA, exploratory testing, and edge-case validation
- Identify and escalate quality risks, regressions, and blockers
- Report on test coverage, quality metrics, and release readiness
- Collaborate with developers on testability and automated test strategy

### Goals
- Ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards
- Reduce defects reaching production
- Enable fast, confident releases with high quality assurance
- Build quality culture across the team

### Typical Communication
- QA planning meetings and test strategy discussions
- Bug reports and quality dashboards
- Sprint reviews and release readiness gate assessments
- Collaboration with developers on testability improvements
- Quality metrics and trend reporting

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Partners on testability improvements and validates implementations
- **Product Managers**: Ensures acceptance criteria clarity and prioritizes test scenarios
- **Project Managers**: Provides release readiness assessments and quality risk escalations

---

## Sponsor / Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide business authority, approve resource allocation, and make strategic decisions that impact project success and organizational priorities. They serve as the executive champion and decision-maker for projects.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter, resource plan, and budget allocation
- Make final trade-off decisions between scope, schedule, and budget
- Remove organizational blockers and facilitate cross-functional alignment
- Serve as the escalation path for business-critical issues and decisions
- Provide strategic direction, success criteria, and business context
- Communicate project status and outcomes to executive leadership

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers business value and ROI
- Maintain executive alignment and stakeholder confidence
- Remove barriers to project success
- Drive organizational priorities through resource allocation

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder briefings and executive updates
- Milestone reviews and decision gate approvals
- Escalation and issue resolution
- Strategic alignment and priority setting conversations
- Budget and resource approval meetings

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: Reviews status, approves plans, and removes organizational obstacles
- **Product Managers**: Aligns on business objectives and success metrics
- **Developers / Technical Lead**: Engages on resource constraints and strategic technology decisions

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove impediments, coach teams on agile practices, and foster a culture of continuous improvement and psychological safety. They enable the team's self-organization and sustainable delivery pace.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove impediments and escalate systemic blockers that hinder team progress
- Mentor the team on agile practices, self-organization, and sprint discipline
- Track and report on team metrics (velocity, burndown, cycle time)
- Coach the team on psychological safety, feedback culture, and continuous improvement
- Work with Project Managers to shield the team from mid-sprint disruptions

### Goals
- Enable the team to deliver consistently and sustainably
- Foster psychological safety and a learning culture
- Reduce impediments and cycle time
- Improve team velocity and predictability through continuous improvement

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and team ceremonies
- Blocker escalation and resolution
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking
- Metrics dashboards and team health check-ins
- One-on-ones and coaching conversations with team members

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Coaches on agile practices, removes blockers, facilitates collaboration
- **Project Managers**: Coordinates on sprint cadence, escalates systemic blockers, provides team health insights
- **Product Managers**: Facilitates backlog refinement and sprint planning collaboration

---

## Communications & Documentation Specialist

### Role Summary
Communications specialists ensure consistent, clear information flow across teams, stakeholders, and users through release notes, documentation, and status updates. They amplify project outcomes and maintain knowledge accessibility.

### Responsibilities
- Draft and publish release notes, feature announcements, and user-facing communications
- Create and maintain knowledge base articles, runbooks, and process documentation
- Coordinate stakeholder communications (status updates, incident notifications, outcomes)
- Maintain project documentation, decision logs, and lessons learned
- Ensure clarity, consistency, and accessibility across all project communications
- Partner with teams to capture and document tribal knowledge

### Goals
- Enable stakeholders and users to understand project outcomes and changes
- Reduce knowledge silos and tribal knowledge dependencies
- Accelerate onboarding through clear, accessible documentation
- Maintain consistent messaging and communication quality

### Typical Communication
- Release note reviews and publication
- Stakeholder communication planning and distribution
- Documentation contributions and reviews
- Status update preparation and delivery
- Knowledge capture sessions and documentation workshops

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Product Managers**: Collaborates on messaging, outcomes, and user communication
- **Project Managers**: Coordinates on status updates and stakeholder communications
- **Developers / Technical Lead**: Captures technical documentation and architectural decisions
- **All Roles**: Partners to document processes, decisions, and lessons learned

- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
